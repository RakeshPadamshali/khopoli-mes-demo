Selected order only —
Campaign coils are the balance-to-produce of open orders sequenced by the planner over a 10-day horizon (coating and thickness family on CGL, light → dark colour on CCL, gauge on CRM, knife set on the slitter, changeover minutes between families); they get a production order and an HR coil at release. Recorded coils keep their windows.
Coil plan — every coil of the line load in sequence, with its route and the planned schedule per step
Same dataset as the full-load gantt: recorded coils (history + plan) and campaign coils (plan only, HR coil at release), ordered by the start of their next open step. The route comes from the chosen FP / SAP route and its sequence is not user-reorderable; click a row for the operation schedule — planned start and end per step, duration, wait before the step, reroutes and shifts. Steps of a WIP coil done before the plan and steps beyond the 10-day horizon are shown greyed.

Order clubbing — small & leftover orders onto one mother coil / campaign
Rules: same product · grade · thickness · coating · paint / RAL → slit-width clubbing packs narrow orders side by side on a 1250 / 1500 mm mother coil (20 mm trim); weight clubbing groups full-width small orders into one campaign so the mother coil meets the 20 t minimum.
Schedule lines
Planning-released schedule lines are confirmed by Operations before charging. Reroute moves a planned coil to the alternate work centre (equipment loading: Slitter ↔ Rewinding + Trimming) or raises a rework production order on the same line; the load impact on both lines is shown. Sequencing rules per line: CGL by width / thickness family, CCL light → dark colour, CRM by gauge.
