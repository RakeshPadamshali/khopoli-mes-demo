Selected order only —
Campaign coils are the balance-to-produce of open orders sequenced by the planner over a 10-day horizon (coating and thickness family on CGL, light → dark colour on CCL, gauge on CRM, knife set on the slitter, changeover minutes between families); they get a production order and an HR coil at release. Recorded coils keep their windows. The Coil Plan report lists every coil of this load in sequence with its route and the planned schedule per step.

Schedule lines
Planning-released schedule lines are confirmed by Operations before charging. Reroute moves a planned coil to the alternate work centre (equipment loading: Slitter ↔ Rewinding + Trimming) or raises a rework production order on the same line; the load impact on both lines is shown. Sequencing rules per line: CGL by width / thickness family, CCL light → dark colour, CRM by gauge.
