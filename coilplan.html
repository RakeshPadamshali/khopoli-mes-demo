Coil plan — one row per coil of the line load, ordered by the start of its next open step
Same dataset as the full-load gantt on Routes & Schedules: recorded coils (history + plan, one row follows the coil through its identities HR coil → HRPO → CRFH → GI → PPGI → slit → pack) and campaign coils (plan only — a production order and an HR coil are assigned at release). The route comes from the chosen FP / SAP route and its sequence is not user-reorderable. Click a row for the operation schedule: planned start and end per step, duration, wait before the step, reroutes and shifts recorded on Routes & Schedules. Steps of a WIP coil done before the plan and steps beyond the 10-day horizon are shown greyed.
