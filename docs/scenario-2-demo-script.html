Presenter script for the Khopoli NextGen MES product demo · Annexure A scenario 2 · 30 minutes · RFP sections 3.1.2–3.1.5
Demo: https://rakeshpadamshali.github.io/khopoli-mes-demo/ As-of moment: the date in the demo header, 10:30, shift A (the dataset is regenerated to the current date) Rush order: 4213090031/10 — Kestrel Auto Components, coil CRF-KHP-2609-0024 waiting for CGL Swap pair: GIC-KHP-2609-0012 ↔ GIC-KHP-2609-0014 (Vega Consumer Durables ↔ Pinnacle Sheet Metal) Reroute coil: PPG-KHP-2609-0013 — Vega Consumer Durables, on the Slitter Data: fictional; lines, products and rules from the RFP

Before you start (2 minutes)

  • Open the demo and click Reset demo in the header. Open two tabs from the sidebar: Routes & Schedules (steps 1, 2 and 4) and Material Allocator (step 3); keep Sales Orders & TDC handy for the consequences.
  • Frame the moment: it is 10:30 on the as-of day and the PPC desk is working the CGL, colour-coating and slitter queues for the next few days. SAP feeds orders, APS sends priority flags, the MES owns the line sequence, the batch allocation and the production orders.
  • Every action is live and stays consistent across pages (kept in the browser). Before the demo day, run python tools/generate_data.py and push, so the dates move to the current date. It is the same dataset as Scenario 1: the orders, coils and lines here are the ones the golden thread runs through.

1Simulate 5–6 small / leftover sales orders eligible for clubbing (length / slit / weight based) — live demo Routes & Schedules → card Order clubbing — small / leftover orders. Click Simulate 6 leftover orders (SAP feed). Then click Accept on the GI 1.2 mm slit-width proposal, and optionally on the PPGI RAL 9002 one. The book already holds 9 open items of 60 t or less, giving two weight / campaign proposals (CRFH 0.6 mm 60 + 40 t; GI 1.2 mm 60 + 60 t, full width, one CGL campaign). The feed adds six small items on three new orders: Orbit Metal Forming (GI 1.2 mm, 300 and 450 mm), Sunbeam Panels (PPGI 0.45 mm RAL 9002, 400 and 380 mm), Blue Ridge Infra (GI 1.2 mm, 480 and 250 mm). Grouping by product, grade, thickness, coating, paint and RAL adds two slit-width proposals: a 1500 mm mother coil cut 480 + 450 + 300 + 250 with 20 mm trim for all four GI items (51 t, three mother coils, Slitter) and a 1000 mm coil cut 400 + 380 for the Sunbeam items (17 t, balance strip to stock). Accept creates the internal sales order for the mother coil, attaches multi-slit plan MSP-2609-nnn to each order line, adds the campaign to the line and logs the plan to SAP; the KPI Clubbing · reroutes counts it.
2Trigger a rush order flag from APS and show it re-prioritizing the schedule Same page, card APS rush flag. Click Show sequence before flag, then Apply rush flag. Optionally open Sales Orders & TDC and point at the RUSH badge on order 4213090031. The flag arrived yesterday at 09:00 through the APS interface (IF-APS-RUSH) with the reason "customer line stoppage at Chakan"; the order priority became 1. Before: the planning-released CGL sequence has Kestrel's coil CRF-KHP-2609-0024 in slot 3, behind Vega Consumer Durables and Orbit Metal Forming. After: the rush coil moves up two slots to slot 1 and takes the first free CGL window; the two other coils shift later by one slot each. Say that the re-sequenced schedule is confirmed back to L2 through the schedule interface and that the full-load gantt above shows the rush bar with the orange outline on the CGL lane, right after the running coil; the campaign coils of the load plan fill the windows between the released coils. The KPI Rush orders reads 1 · APS flag applied.
3Force a batch swap between two sales orders mid-process and show the BTA / BTP update Material Allocator → card Batch swap between sales orders — BTA / BTP update. Click Show before swap, then Apply swap. Two galvanized coils of the identical TDC (DX51D+Z 0.80 × 1250, Z120): GIC-KHP-2609-0012 (20.85 t) was allocated to Vega Consumer Durables 4213090008/10 (100 t, required 15 Sep) and GIC-KHP-2609-0014 (17.51 t) to Pinnacle Sheet Metal 4213090012/10 (80 t, required 9 Sep). PPC swaps them because Pinnacle has the earlier delivery date and needs the heavier coil. The balance-to-allocate moves with the coils: Pinnacle 62.49 → 59.15 t, Vega 79.15 → 82.49 t; balance-to-produce does not change, because nothing new was produced. Both allocation records keep the previous item (ALC-SW-0012, ALC-SW-0014), the coils keep their genealogy, and the batch re-allocation message IF-SAP-15 posted to SAP with status OK. The fulfilment bars on the Sales Orders page follow the same numbers.
4Alter / reroute a batch to a different line (equipment loading change) and show the rework production order generated automatically Back on Routes & Schedules, card Schedule lines (line selector on Slitter). Type 0013 in the search box to find coil PPG-KHP-2609-0013 (Vega Consumer Durables) and click Reroute. Keep the option Reroute to Rewinding + Trimming Line and read the impact boxes, then click Apply. The route master lists the Rewinding + Trimming Line as the alternate work centre for slitting and trimming (ten routes carry it), so the planner may move a coil when the slitter is loaded. The impact panel shows the equipment loading change before it is applied, over the 10-day horizon: Slitter 52 h → 51 h, Rewinding + Trimming 15 h → 16 h, and the rework production order PO-KHP-2609-R001 that will be generated automatically while the original PO-KHP-2609-0115 is closed short. After Apply the line selector jumps to the Rewinding + Trimming Line: the coil sits there in the first gap that fits it, with the new production order (the old one shown as "was …") and the status REROUTE from SLT; the slitter row is greyed as REROUTED → RWL, the full-load gantt shows a purple bar on the new lane, and the toast confirms the L2 schedule message was re-sent. The KPI now reads 1 · 1 rework PO. Say that the rework order carries the updated route and yield string of the new work centre.

Optional extras (if time allows)

  • Same-line rework. Switch the line selector to Continuous Galvanizing Line, search 0026, click Reroute on the Apex Appliances coil CRF-KHP-2609-0026 and choose Rework — re-process on Continuous Galvanizing Line: rework order PO-KHP-2609-R002 is sequenced after the current campaign and the CGL load over the horizon rises from 199 h to 200 h.
  • Full line load. Scroll back to the gantt section: the full-load view shows the campaign plan on every line with loading percentages under it, and the selected-order view shows only the chosen order across its route steps, including its campaign coils and any coil beyond the horizon.
  • Operations confirmation. Click Confirm on any open schedule line: the confirmation timestamp appears and the schedule message goes to L2 through the middleware.
  • Where it lands. The Plant Dashboard shows the running batches and the behind-schedule count; the Integration Monitor lists the SAP and L2 interfaces used in this scenario (order feed, batch re-allocation IF-SAP-15, schedule to L2).

Tips

Order of steps. Steps 1, 2 and 4 are on the same page, top to bottom: clubbing card, rush card, line schedule with gantt. Step 3 is on the Material Allocator. The order of the steps does not matter to the demo state.
Proposal ids are stable. A proposal keeps its id when more orders arrive, so an accepted proposal never changes its label mid-demo.
Timing. Step 1 about 8 min · step 2 about 5 min · step 3 about 7 min · step 4 about 10 min.
Reset between runs. Live actions stay in the browser; click Reset demo before the next audience, otherwise the accepted proposals, the reroutes and the swap state from the last run are still there.

Honesty notes

The clubbing engine is a demo heuristic: first-fit of slit widths into standard mother-coil widths (1000 / 1220 / 1250 / 1500 mm) with 20 mm edge trim, and campaign clubbing of full-width items with an identical spec. The real product would use the planning engine's rules and the customer's mother-coil catalogue.
The APS rush flag and the batch swap are pre-computed in the dataset (before and after sequences, BTA before and after); the buttons replay them. The six leftover orders are simulated in the page and are not part of the JSON data feed for the chatbot.
Rework production orders and reroute times are generated in the browser: the coil is placed after the last open job on the alternate line (plus 30 minutes) or at its original time if that is later. No SAP or L2 round trip happens in the static demo.
All customers, orders, coils and people are fictional; line names, products, rules and the interfaces come from the RFP.
