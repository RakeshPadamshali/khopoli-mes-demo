Demo: https://rakeshpadamshali.github.io/khopoli-mes-demo/ As-of moment: the date in the demo header, 10:30, shift A (the dataset is regenerated to the current date) Hero order: 4213090017/10 — Apex Appliances, DX51D+Z 0.50 × 1220, Z120, RMP RAL 9002, 120 t Hero HR coil: HRC-VJ-2608-0471 — Vijayanagar heat H26-VJ-7731 / slab SLB-VJ-7731-02 RFP references: §3.1–3.2, 3.2.4, 4.1–4.3 Data: fictional (customers, orders, coils); lines, products and rules from the RFP

Before you start (2 minutes)

  • Open the demo and click Reset demo in the header. Then open the tabs from the sidebar in this order: Sales Orders & TDC, Routes & Schedules, Material Allocator, Shop-floor Execution, Quality & Defects, Slitting · Packing · Dispatch, Genealogy, Integration Monitor. Tabs keep their state, so you can jump back and forth.
  • Frame the moment: it is 10:30 on the as-of day shown in the header. The hero coil has already been pickled, cold-rolled and galvanized (that history is on screen); it came off the colour-coating line at 01:40 this morning. The demo performs the remaining steps live.
  • Before the demo day, run python tools/generate_data.py and push: every date in the dataset moves to the current date while ids and the story stay the same.
  • One dataset runs through every step: the same order, coil and defects appear on every page, as Annexure A requires.

The ten Annexure steps

#Annexure stepPage and actionWhat to say / show
1Load a sales order (or simulate SAP order feed) for a GI coil order with a TDC Sales Orders & TDC → click Simulate SAP order feed (new order 4213090041/10 arrives with a NEW · IDoc badge). Expand the flagged hero row 4213090017/10. The TDC comes from SAP variant configuration: grade DX51D+Z, thickness 0.50 ±0.03, width +5/−0, coating Z120 with band 120–138 g/m², paint RMP RAL 9002, DFT 20 ±3 / 7 ±2 µm, slitting 610 + 610, packing, EN 10204 3.1 certificate, marking. Point at the schedule lines and the fulfilment bar (21.4 t allocated, 19.3 t produced, BTA 98.6 t).
2Factory Planner output at schedule-line level, alongside the manually entered SAP route order Routes & Schedules (hero item is preselected). Left card = SAP route order (manual). Right card = Factory Planner MFG order FPM-4213090017-10 with process path PKL › CRM › CGL › CCL › SLT › PKG, yield string, route string, material tree and the schedule line. Click Use SAP route / Use FP route to show the planner's choice. Below, the full-load gantt shows every line's campaign plan with the hero bars outlined in red and cyan, and the selected-order gantt shows only this order's coils across its route steps; the schedule table has Operations Confirm buttons.
3Allocate a free-stock HR coil batch to the order via a Material Allocator suggestion Material Allocator (hero item is first in the list). The panel shows the coil already allocated a week ago from an MA suggestion, plus three ranked free HR coils for the remaining 98.6 t with score and reasons (grade family, width with trim, reduction feasible, FIFO age). Two-step authorisation: click PPC approve, then QC approve, then Allocate on rank 1. The allocation log, yard status and BTA update; the reallocation message goes to SAP.
4Charge the batch at Pickling, then CRM — PDI generation and PDO confirmation via middleware Shop-floor Execution (opens on the Pickling Line tab; the Action column is first). Row 1 is running: click Receive PDO, then Confirm production → Post to SAP. Row 2 becomes chargeable: click Charge. Repeat on the Cold Roll Mill tab. Charging generates the PDI (targets JSON) and shows the L2 acknowledgement; the PDO brings the actuals back and the confirmation posts the mass balance to SAP. In the “PDO vs PDI” dropdown pick the ★ entries for the hero coil's own history: PKL HRC-VJ-2608-0471 → HPO-KHP-2609-0018 (acid 13.0 %), CRM HPO → CRF-KHP-2609-0018 (exit 0.49 mm). Say that every message travels MES → middleware → L2 gateway, never point to point.
5Move the CR full-hard coil to CGL — genealogy link from HR coil forward Still on Shop-floor: CGL tab, dropdown entry CRF-KHP-2609-0018 → GIC-KHP-2609-0017 (three days ago). The input and output coil ids are the consumesInput link. Optionally flash the Genealogy tab: HRC → HPO → CRF → GIC on one line.
6At CGL: zinc coating-weight target-band check and auto-comparison of PDO actuals vs PDI targets, deviation flagged Same CGL comparison table. For a hard deviation pick PDO-CGL-0016 in the dropdown. Hero coil: target 126 g/m² in band 120–138, measured 126.8 total (63.6 / 63.2), zinc bath Al 0.21 within 0.18–0.24; line speed flagged as a throughput warning only. PDO-CGL-0016: coating weight below the 120 band minimum, flagged as a deviation, and the note says the output coil goes on parameter-based hold pending a quality decision.
7Move to CCL — apply the paint / DFT spec, simulate a DFT test result outside spec Quality & Defects. The hero certificate shows DFT top and back PENDING and the dispatch gate closed. Enter 16.8 and 7.1, click Record result & evaluate. 16.8 µm is below the 17.0 minimum (20 ±3): certificate FAIL, dispatch gate blocked, the auto-flagged defect DEF-2609-0003 is confirmed, and the downgrade suggestion appears with four candidate orders validated parameter by parameter. Sunbeam Panels 4213090024/10 (same spec, DFT 15 ±3) accepts the coil. Click Re-allocate coil to this order, or show the override (QC Head) and rework options as the manual workflow.
8Slit and pack the coil — packing movement and vendor-wise packing bill Slitting · Packing · Dispatch (hero card at the top). Click Run slitting plan, then Pack & print label. The plan cuts 1220 mm into 610 + 610 (children SLC-KHP-2609-0032 / 0033) with the genealogy link and scrap posting. Pack PK-KHP-2609-0018 (18.37 t, Shree Packers, domestic VCI + stretch) gets a QR label; the packing movement posts the storage-location change and the vendor bill table gains the current week's line.
9Dispatch the batch — show the dispatch message structure that goes to SAP Same page: click Dispatch & send message to SAP. The JSON on the right is the DELVRY07 / SHPCON structure sent through the middleware: header (plant, dispatch id, order and schedule line, vehicle MH-12-KT-4471), items (batch, material, quantity, coils, quality status CLEARED), totals and correlation id. Dispatched-batch information returns from SAP.
10Full genealogy of the finished coil back to HR coil / slab / heat Genealogy · Digital Thread (link from the packing page or the sidebar; hero coil is preselected). The thread reads heat H26-VJ-7731 → slab SLB-VJ-7731-02 → HRC-VJ-2608-0471 → HPO → CRF → GIC → PPG → two slit coils → pack → dispatch, plus the allocatedTo order node. Click any node for its attributes and the ontology triples (consumesInput, producedOn, conformsTo, allocatedTo, detectedOn, attributedTo). Click show propagation on a defect to close with the Scenario 4 hook.

Tips

Middleware proof. In the Integration Monitor, search 4213090017: five messages for this order (PDI, PDO, production confirmation, batch attributes, paint posting) with latency and history.
Dispatch is gated. The slit, pack and dispatch buttons stay disabled until a quality decision is recorded in step 7. Say this out loud — it is the graph-based dispatch gate the RFP asks for.
More than one order. The certificate selector on Quality lists two more colour-coated coils with a pending DFT result (a Sunbeam Panels export coil and a Vega Consumer Durables coil), and the coil selector on Packing lists every finished coil awaiting packing, four in all. Repeat steps 7–9 on one of them for the PASS path: in-spec defaults, gate opens, pack and dispatch without a quality decision. Each running line also carries a different sales order.
Timing. Steps 1–3 about 15 min · steps 4–6 about 15 min · steps 7–10 about 20 min.

Honesty notes

The hero coil's pickling to galvanizing is shown as history plus the PDI/PDO comparison; the live charge and PDO clicks happen on the coil currently on each line.
Live-demo actions are kept in the browser and stay consistent across pages; click Reset demo between runs, otherwise the previous run's DFT result and dispatch are still there.
All customers, orders, coils, people and incidents are fictional; line names, products, rules and the ontology come from the RFP.
