Step 2 · Candidate pool — open sales-order items of 60 t or less, grouped by spec family
A spec family = same product, grade, thickness, coating, paint system and RAL. Only items of one family can share a mother coil or a campaign. Items marked narrow (760 mm or less) are slit-width candidates; full-width items are length or campaign candidates. An item with no partner in its family stays a single-order coil.

Clubbing rules used by the proposals
SLIT-WIDTH narrow orders side by side
  • Same spec family, each strip 760 mm or less
  • First-fit onto a standard mother coil: 1000 · 1220 · 1250 · 1500 mm, 20 mm edge trim
  • Smallest standard width that fits; the rest is a balance strip to stock
  • One mother coil per ≈ 20 t of clubbed tonnage; slit on the Slitter
LENGTH one coil cut into lengths
  • Same spec family and the same full width
  • Each order below 20 t, together 24 t or less
  • One mother coil, cut by length or weight per order on the Rewinding + Trimming line
  • Each order line gets its share (t and metres) on the multi-slit plan
WEIGHT / CAMPAIGN full-width orders in one run
  • Same spec family, full width, too heavy for one coil
  • Sequenced back to back as one campaign on the coating line (CGL, CCL for colour, CRM for full-hard, PKL for pickled)
  • One mother coil per ≈ 20 t; the changeover between them is saved
  • Every order keeps its own coils and identity
